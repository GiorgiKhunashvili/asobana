# Place facts (2026-10-17)

One short "იცოდი?" fact per journey stop (review item 11), in `placeFacts`
(`mobile/src/content/destinations.ts`). They appear on a stop's sheet on the path
(once the player has arrived), on the victory screen of the stop's first level and
on the postcard page, written on a note under the stamp.

- **Status: approved by the owner on 2026-10-17.** The Georgian was written by
  Claude and has had no separate native-speaker review. New or changed facts need
  the same checking and the owner's approval.
- **Checking.** Each claim was checked against at least one source, read on the page
  (search snippets don't count). UNESCO and Britannica pages refused automated reads
  (HTTP 403), so the sources are Wikipedia, official tourism or government sites and
  the Georgian National Encyclopedia. Fifteen first drafts were reworded or replaced
  after checking; the notes column says how.
- **Style.** One sentence, no shaky dates or numbers, hedged where the source hedges
  ("გადმოცემით", "ლეგენდის მიხედვით", "ერთ-ერთი"). Each fact differs from the
  stop's postcard caption.
