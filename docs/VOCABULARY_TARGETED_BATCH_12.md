# Everyday vocabulary review — targeted batch 12

**Owner decision, 2026-10-22: all 83 candidates rejected.** None is approved; all are excluded from required answers, variants and bonuses (`owner-targeted-batch-12`). The review notes below are kept as the record. 83 candidates aimed at lowering answer repetition. Active revision 29 is unchanged: 2,318 approved words, 500 levels, 21.84% repeated answer slots.

**Why short words:** 467 of revision 29's 546 repeated slots are 3- and 4-letter connector words (62 three-letter and 520 four-letter approvals). **But the unreviewed pool of familiar short words is nearly exhausted.** Of about 1,800 everyday words brainstormed by topic, only 82 three-to-five-letter spellings had never been recorded; corpus and Wiktionary scans added mostly verbal nouns, inflected forms, names and rare words. So this batch has 83 candidates instead of about 100: 3 with 3 letters, 21 with 4 and 59 with 5. I did not pad it with obscure words.

## How to review

- **Tier A** (30) = everyday; **Tier B** (53) = familiar but regional, bookish, rural or specialised. Tiers are my judgment, not native-speaker review.
- **22 verbal nouns** (marked VN), such as ფიქრი, რჩევა, ცეკვა, are proposed only for a concept or object sense (thought, advice, a dance). The verbal-action rule excludes action senses (წერა, ჭამა, კოცნა); revision 29 already approves similar concept nouns such as დავა, ცოდნა and ჩვევა. Reject them as a group if you see them as actions.
- Ranked by how many usable 5-, 6- and 7-tile wheels of the approved vocabulary contain the word (more wheels = more boards it can join). Wheels count only unions of two already-approved words; a candidate can also anchor new wheels, so a low count does not mean it is unusable. "Trial uses" is how often the planner used it in 500 levels.
- 107 more spellings were screened out before this list (action senses, serious illnesses, barbarisms, spelling variants, no source, uncommon terms). They are listed in the JSON with reasons; they are not owner decisions.
