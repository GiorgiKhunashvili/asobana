# Targeted vocabulary batch 17 — owner reviewed

The other **70 were not selected** and remain unapproved for required use, without an explicit all-gameplay exclusion. The earlier filter removed 11 of the original 100 candidates.

Removed categories:

- Six ordinal numerals: მესამე, მეოთხე, მეხუთე, მეცხრე, მეასე and მეოცე.
- Both approximate-colour adjectives: მორუხო and მოშავო.
- Three analogous prefixed adjectives: უდედო, უმამო and უმზეო. These are an editorial application of the owner's request; the owner did not individually name these three spellings.

მეორე was absent from this batch and was already excluded by targeted batch 05. Its prior exclusion is preserved. The remaining ordinary adjectives are retained for individual review; this request is not treated as a ban on all adjectives.

The owner's broader familiarity criterion remains: familiar school, literary, historical, cultural and other vocabulary may be reviewed without being strictly everyday words. All existing length, grammar, name, plural, barbarism and individual decisions remain.

Total approvals are **2,358**, including **52 awaiting integration** from batches 13–15 and 17. Batch 16 remains pending in full. Active revision 30 retains 2,306 required approvals and 500 levels. No gameplay content was changed. The separate 39-word approved-pool audit remains unchanged and awaits owner review.
