# Everyday vocabulary review — revision 12

Reviewed all **250 required answers** from revision 11. **88 are now bonus-only**;
162 remain eligible. Rebuilding the crossings replaced 135 required spellings in
total (the 88 demoted words plus 47 eligible words that no longer fit the new boards).
The final campaign still has **250 unique required answers**.

## Review standard and evidence

Required answers use an explicit, sense-based allowlist of 778 familiar nouns,
adjectives and numbers from the existing reference dictionary. Short spelling
and dictionary inclusion no longer qualify a word automatically. Less familiar
species, specialist tools/materials, literary alternatives and ambiguous senses
were conservatively removed from required play. This is an assistant editorial
review, not a native-speaker survey or a claim that every player knows every word.
All 250 replacement answers received a final sense review after board selection.

The [Spoken Georgian Frequency List v2026.07](https://easygeorgian.com/spoken-georgian-frequency/)
by Lasse N. and Tamar N., EasyGeorgian Research, supplies the supporting ranks below
(CC BY 4.0; ranks extracted and joined to our existing dictionary, no raw posts or
subtitle text). It is a forum/subtitle proxy with AI lemmatization, topic bias and
possible homograph effects. Missing ranks do not prove rarity; household words
can be underrepresented. We used editorial meaning review first, frequency second.
Glosses retain their existing Wiktionary attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 provenance in
[the reference dictionary](../content/dictionaries/discovery-v9-ka.json).

The owner's earlier clarification still applies: name-only entries are excluded;
ordinary meanings of name homographs (e.g. ნელი = slow, იმედი = hope) remain eligible.
Pronouns, excluded verb/action forms and name-only spellings remain blocked for both
required and bonus play. Valid uncommon words, including ურცი, რაზა and თელა, remain
in the bonus vocabulary, recognized whenever the available letters can form them.
They need not appear on a particular board and never block level completion.

The allowlist is enforced in both the Python rebuild and TypeScript release gate,
including accepted answer variants. The wider playable dictionary stays at 1,202
words. The campaign has 74 five-letter and 21 six-letter answers; longer answers
still start at levels 3 and 18. Levels 1–5 use five tiles, 6–25 six tiles, and 26–50
seven tiles. Seven tiles permit familiar crossing combinations with unique answers;
required answers still have at most six letters. All boards retain wide readable
layouts and every chapter retains its challenge/recovery sequence.
