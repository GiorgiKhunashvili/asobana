## Review and provenance

All 1,475 required slots and canonical-only accepted variants were checked against the 1,997-word owner allowlist and individual source-linked senses. All eligible formable bonus words were compared against the 2,115-word playable dictionary. All 93 explicit exclusions and 41 unselected alternatives are absent. Existing rare-word and plural reservations remain. The approved draft SHA-256 is recorded in `content/levels/discovery.v27.review.json`; promotion used the official rebuild script after this audit.

The [October ledger](VOCABULARY_EXPANSION_REVIEW_2026_10_20.json) records every new approval and rejection. The [full checklist](VOCABULARY_OWNER_REVIEW_V27.md) includes meanings and usage for all approvals and bonus-only words. The [trial report](VOCABULARY_GENERATION_TRIAL_2026_10_20.md) records search details and per-addition sources.

The owner waived preservation of existing player progress for this release. The existing migration route was extended without adding a destructive reset.
