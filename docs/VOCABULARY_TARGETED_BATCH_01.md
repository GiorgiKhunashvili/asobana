# Targeted vocabulary review — batch 01

**Integration update:** All approved entries in this batch are now included in revision 28 (345 levels). The pending-release descriptions below record the earlier review stage.

**100 new candidates: 5 three-letter, 45 four-letter and 50 five-letter words.** The owner approved **54** and left **46 unapproved**. The total approved pool is now **2,051**, exceeding the 2,000-word goal by 51. Revision 27 still bundles 1,997 approvals; the 54 additions await release integration.

Each candidate has an independent hypothetical board with four already-approved words, including at least one currently unused approval. Across these examples, **108 distinct unused approvals** appear (60 six-letter, 45 five-letter and three four-letter words). These are alternative examples, not a claim that 100 new levels can be appended together. No campaign repetition reduction is claimed before approval and joint optimization.

The original suggested 15/60/25 length split was a planning estimate. This batch uses the stronger verified 5/45/50 mix. The owner explicitly approved the selected senses, including the literary-labelled აისი. Unselected words remain unapproved; they are not automatically classified as explicit all-gameplay exclusions.
